# Gwinnett & Beyond Weekly — Master Newsletter Prompt

Create a warm, polished, neighborhood-focused weekly email for Danny Emmett, Emmett Realty Group, Keller Williams Realty. The primary geography is Gwinnett County and surrounding Georgia counties.

## Identity

- Name: `Gwinnett & Beyond Weekly`
- Subject: `A Short Weekly Update`
- Subtitle: `A short weekly update for homeowners, friends, and neighbors across Gwinnett and the surrounding counties.`

## Permanent structure

1. Header and issue date
2. Danny's Note
3. Gwinnett & Beyond Market Snapshot
4. Two rotating content features
5. Cartoon Corner
6. Referral / Share section
7. Contact / Action area
8. Footer / compliance

## Rotation

- Week 1: Local Business Spotlight; Homeowner / Seasonal Home Tip
- Week 2: Recipe; Local Community Event
- Week 3: Local History; Seasonal Home Maintenance
- Week 4: Restaurant / Coffee Shop / Community Spotlight; Weekend Idea
- Fifth Wednesday: choose a non-repetitive local/community/lifestyle combination.

Never include a monthly quiz, gift-card entry, winner announcement/reminder, Cathy Jordan winner logic, or an automatic assistant handoff.

## Editorial and QA rules

- Match Danny's Note to the issue date and only mention feature types actually present.
- Research market data and factual local claims from authoritative sources. Never invent figures.
- Reject stale date language, visible placeholders, “Needs verification,” stale internal notes, and internal Command checklist wording in subscriber HTML.
- When there is no cartoon image URL, use a clean text-only fallback; never show an image placeholder.
- Keep internal compliance reminders in the approval email only.

## Output and safety

Produce complete email-safe HTML from `<!DOCTYPE html>` through `</html>` in a fenced `html` block when automation requests copyable source, and save a separate versioned `.html` file. Use an approximately 600px table-based layout, inline CSS, safe fonts, responsive sizing, no JavaScript, and no external CSS.

The Monday workflow prepares the coming Wednesday issue, generates and verifies HTML, and prepares approval copy for Danny. It never distributes to subscribers and never automatically hands off to an assistant. Approval ends at `APPROVED`; handoff is a separate explicit future action.
